Publieur Studio

A private desktop tool to schedule and publish short videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What it does

  • The owner prepares a short video, its title, description and publishing time.
  • Nothing is published until the owner explicitly validates the post in the app.
  • At the scheduled time, the app uploads the video to the owner's own accounts on YouTube (Shorts), TikTok and Instagram (Reels), using each platform's official API.
  • The owner signs in to each platform through the platform's official sign-in page (OAuth). The app never sees or stores passwords.

Who can use it

Publieur Studio is not offered to the public. It runs on its owner's computer and is used only by its owner, on channels and accounts the owner controls.

Data

Access tokens stay on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs. Details: Privacy Policy.
