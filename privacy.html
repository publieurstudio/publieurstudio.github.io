Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Publieur Studio ("the app") is a private desktop tool run on its owner's computer. It is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, TikTok account and Instagram account.

1. Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (scope youtube.upload) and read-only access to the channel's basic information and the status of uploaded videos (scope youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, open ID, avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram (Meta): basic account information and permission to publish Reels to the owner's professional account.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the videos the owner has validated, with the title, description and settings the owner chose, and to show the owner the result (link and status of each post). It is not used for any other purpose, including advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a folder restricted to the owner's user account. They are never sent anywhere except to the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. The app has no server, no database in the cloud and no analytics.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to any third party.

5. Google API Services

Publieur Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it, the owner also agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service and to the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects an account in the app or deletes the app's folder. Access can also be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok in Settings › Security › Manage app permissions, Instagram in Settings › Apps and websites.
